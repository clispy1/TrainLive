Phase 05
Content Production

Record all videos before the challenge goes live.

    Recruitment ad, VSL, Day 0, Day 1–14 dailies, winner announcement.

Phase 06
Creator Recruitment

Fill 50 spots before the challenge launches.

  • Post recruitment ad on TikTok and Instagram

    If not met, refund all entry fees as promised.
Phase 07
The Challenge (Day 0–14)

Run it. Keep the energy high every single day.

  • Day 0 — Send product, affiliate links, rules to all creators

  • Days 1–14 — Daily video, leaderboard update, group active
    Challenge Manager owns this daily.
  • Track sales and performance throughout Tech
  • Day 14 — Tally final scores, confirm winners
  • Announce winners publicly on all platforms
    Make it big. This content automatically recruits round two creators.
  • Pay winners and team Money
  • Begin investor repayment from revenue Money
Phase 08
Post-Challenge Review

Learn, document, and prepare for round two.

  • Review total revenue, costs, and profit
  • Document what worked and what didn't
  • Collect testimonials from top creators
    Social proof for round two and future investors.
  • Begin planning round two — product, niche, business partner
